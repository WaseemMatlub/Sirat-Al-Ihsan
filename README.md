# Ihsan

Learn your deen, year by year. A Hanafi, Maturidi syllabus with Arabic at its core.

**Live site:** https://waseemmatlub.github.io/ihsan/

## How it works

Each year has three parts, and the next year unlocks only when all three are done:

1. **Books**: read core texts line by line in Arabic and English, then check your understanding.
2. **Flashcards**: spaced repetition, like Anki. Cards return just before you would forget them.
3. **Final test**: 40 questions from the year's books and flashcards. Score 80% or more to unlock the next year.

## Year 1: Foundations

- **Qur'an words**: the 500 most frequent words, about 86% of the Qur'an's text, ordered using Quranic Arabic Corpus data
- **Nur al-Idah**: Hanafi fiqh of purification, prayer and fasting (1,083 cards, 22 chapters)
- **Al-'Aqidah al-Tahawiyyah** and **Umm al-Barahin**

Years 2 and 3 are planned and will be added as content is prepared.
